## Architecture findings preserved by the cycle

### Surviving/narrowed propositions

- translation should preserve tested invariants rather than map vocabulary by surface resemblance;
- functional similarity is one equivalence dimension, not semantic identity;
- segmentation remains a hypothesis when channel boundaries are not semantically guaranteed;
- context dependencies should be typed and intervention-tested;
- competing interpretations and ambiguity should remain representable;
- semantic content, provenance, evidential state, action priority, and permission/authorization should not silently collapse;
- interactionally supported common ground is separate from private-state claims;
- repair-like behavior is not proof of understanding;
- partner-specific conventions can be real while remaining scope-limited;
- silence/absence requires an evidenced expectation relation before it receives communicative meaning;
- human pragmatic categories are adversarial/control families, not species-neutral primitives.

### Preserved rejected overclaims

The research notes explicitly reject or narrow claims including:

- `FUNCTION_IS_MORE_UNIVERSAL_THAN_FORM`;
- provable symmetric recursive mutual belief as a requirement for operational common ground;
- repair behavior as proof of intentional communication;
- task success as proof of shared semantics;
- compositionality as an automatic certificate of generalization;
- flat context as an adequate semantic representation;
- human speech-act labels as species-neutral ontology.
