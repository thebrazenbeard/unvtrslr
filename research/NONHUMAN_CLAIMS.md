# Nonhuman Communication Claims Supplement

Status: `PASS_3_CLAIMS_SUPPLEMENT / AUTHORITATIVE_FOR_PASS_3`

This supplement extends `research/CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md` for the nonhuman communication pass while preserving Pass 3 as an independently reviewable evidence layer. It is intentionally kept as a supplement rather than silently renumbering or rewriting the earlier human/R1-R2 ledger.

## NH-C01 — Playback/intervention can support reference-like nonhuman communication claims beyond context correlation

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / SPECIES-BOUNDED`

Vervet alarm playbacks, dolphin signature-whistle playbacks, Japanese great-tit sequence playbacks, elephant receiver-specific call playbacks, and honeybee recruitment/navigation experiments all provide causal or intervention-style evidence stronger than corpus co-occurrence alone.

**Project consequence:** semantic qualification should rank playback/intervention evidence above passive signal-context correlation when safe and ecologically valid.

**Ceiling:** none of these results establishes a general animal lexicon or human-equivalent semantics.

## NH-C02 — Rich signal structure does not imply known semantics

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / PROJECT_BOUNDARY`

Sperm-whale coda research demonstrates contextual and combinatorial acoustic structure while explicitly leaving the communicative function/semantics of many features unresolved.

**Project consequence:** `STRUCTURE_DISCOVERED` and `SEMANTICS_GROUNDED` must be different qualification states.

## NH-C03 — Combination must be decomposed into structural and semantic evidence levels

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / DESIGN_REQUIREMENT`

Comparative systems range from strong bounded playback-supported compositional cases (Japanese tits) to systematic call combination with debated human analogies (Campbell's monkeys) to complex acoustic combination with semantics unresolved (sperm whales).

**Project consequence:** record separately `FORM_COMBINATION`, `RECEIVER_COMBINATION_SENSITIVITY`, `PREDICTABLE_FUNCTION_COMBINATION`, and `COMPOSITIONAL_SEMANTIC_GENERALIZATION`.

## NH-C04 — Individual addressing can be implemented by different mechanisms

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / SPECIES-BOUNDED`

Dolphins can use learned signature-whistle labels; parrots can address through imitation of a target's calls; elephants show receiver-specific name-like calls not explained by simple imitation.

**Project consequence:** do not canonize a universal `NAME` token or mechanism. Represent the tested relation — individual identity/addressing — separately from the signal-generation strategy.

## NH-C05 — Classifier-readable information is not equivalent to receiver meaning

**Status:** `DESIGN_REQUIREMENT / SUPPORTED_WARNING`

Bat vocalizations and many large animal corpora allow machine classifiers to predict caller, addressee, context, or behavior. This shows information is present in the signal but does not by itself establish what receivers represent or what signallers intend.

**Project consequence:** statistical decoding is an intermediate evidence class below causal/behavioral semantic qualification.

## NH-C06 — Turn-like coordination is cross-species, but its mechanism is not guaranteed

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / SPECIES-BOUNDED`

Marmosets show reciprocal vocal timing and overlap avoidance. A larger later model challenged the earlier coupled-oscillator explanation while preserving the behavioral regularity.

**Project consequence:** store observed coordination separately from explanatory mechanism. Do not hard-code one turn-taking dynamical model into signalhood detection.

## NH-C07 — Great-ape gesture provides strong operational intentionality evidence, but intentionality is not a universal communication prerequisite

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / DESIGN_REQUIREMENT`

Audience targeting, response waiting, persistence, elaboration, and attention-sensitive modality choice support goal-directed gestural communication in great apes. Other well-established communication systems do not have comparable evidence.

**Project consequence:** intentionality can raise confidence in signalhood/function where supported but cannot be required across all communicators.

## NH-C08 — Shared physical variables may be represented through species-specific perceptual quantities

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / PROJECT_BOUNDARY`

Honeybee waggle-dance distance information is calibrated through optic-flow experience rather than a simple observer-independent metric distance.

**Project consequence:** distinguish evaluator physical variables from sender/receiver perceptual variables and from learned cross-system invariants.

## NH-C09 — Signal channel and sensory system may be the same physical substrate

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / SPECIES-BOUNDED`

Weakly electric fish use electric organ discharges for both electrolocation and social electrocommunication.

**Project consequence:** `environment`, `sensor stream`, and `communication channel` cannot be universally disjoint architecture components.

## NH-C10 — The same actuator/pattern generator may be communicative in one context and noncommunicative in another

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / SPECIES-BOUNDED`

Cephalopod body-pattern machinery participates in camouflage, defense, courtship, agonistic displays, and other functions.

**Project consequence:** signalhood is a contextual hypothesis over pattern + audience + history + response, not a permanent label on a form.

## NH-C11 — Communication can persist in the environment and be collectively authored

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / DESIGN_REQUIREMENT`

Ant pheromone trails can be deposited and reinforced by multiple workers, persist after individual passage, and guide later recruits.

**Project consequence:** the general theory cannot require one current sender, one current receiver, or a discrete message event. Provenance may be collective, distributed, and decaying.

## NH-C12 — Communication can use electric, chemical, substrate-borne, polarization, and other channels absent from ordinary human interfaces

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / PROJECT_BOUNDARY`

Animal communication research documents electrocommunication, pheromone signaling, substrate-borne vibration, and cephalopod visual/polarization signaling.

**Project consequence:** channel discovery must precede semantic tokenization; a system restricted to human-visible/audible modalities can fail before semantics begin.

## NH-C13 — Social learning and culture shape multiple nonhuman communication systems

**Status:** `SUPPORTED_PRIOR_WORK / SPECIES-BOUNDED`

Relevant examples include sperm-whale vocal clans, songbird cultural transmission, dolphin vocal learning, parrot imitation, and honeybee social learning of dance accuracy.

**Project consequence:** bridge provenance should separate biological constraints, individual learning, social learning, population convention, and transmission history.

## NH-C14 — `Reference` is not one unitary cross-species relation

**Status:** `DESIGN_REQUIREMENT / INFERENCE`

Animal systems show distinct evidence patterns: external predator information, displaced route vectors, individual addressing, social-unit identity cues, and context/addressee information.

**Project consequence:** do not force one universal `REFERENT` node/type. Store the actual tested relation and evidence.

## NH-C15 — `Two agents + shared observable environment` is an experimentally useful subset of a broader coupling problem

**Status:** `PROJECT_HYPOTHESIS / INFERENCE`

Persistent pheromone fields, multi-whale choruses, and sensor/channel fusion expose cases poorly captured by a single dyadic message abstraction.

**Project consequence:** retain the two-agent condition as the first controlled experiment, but generalize theory language toward `two or more coupled adaptive systems with partially overlapping observables and influence surfaces`.

## NH-C16 — Several first-100 families are anthropocentric stress tests rather than likely universal bootstrap primitives

**Status:** `DESIGN_REQUIREMENT / INFERENCE`

Agency/goal/belief, possession, speech-act class, persistent object identity, and explicit counterfactual report are useful evaluator challenges but lack cross-species evidence as universal necessities.

**Project consequence:** allow `NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_OBSERVED_COMMUNICATION_SYSTEM` where a challenge presupposes unsupported ontology rather than counting it as bootstrap failure.

## NH-C17 — Nonhuman evidence supports communication diversity, not a proof of universal translatability

**Status:** `PROJECT_BOUNDARY`

The reviewed literature establishes many partial mechanisms under species-specific ecologies. It does not show that every signal system admits a recoverable mapping to human semantics, that all communicators share a concept inventory, or that interaction can overcome non-identifiability.

**Project consequence:** cross-species evidence narrows the universal kernel and strengthens failure states; it does not prove the interstellar thesis.
