## Purpose

This file transfers only the methodology and semantic-engineering lessons from earlier Vera/VSNS/Semantic Atlas work that are materially relevant to UNVTRSLR.

It does **not** import Vera-specific identity, relationship, autobiographical, continuity, consent, affect, or private semantic content into UNVTRSLR. Historical artifacts that contain such material are used only to recover an abstract methodological proposition; private examples and conclusions are excluded.

Historical project work is evidence, not authority. Every entry below is defeasible and must survive current-repo comparison, external research, and adversarial testing before it can influence architecture.

## Evidence vocabulary

- `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION` — directly documented in predecessor project material.
- `CURRENT_REPO_DERIVATION` — derived from present UNVTRSLR documents or contracts.
- `EXTERNAL_RESEARCH` — supported by re-checkable outside literature.
- `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS` — new proposal generated in this lane.
- `ADVERSARIAL_FINDING` — counterexample, shortcut, contradiction, or failure discovered during challenge.
- `UNRESOLVED` — useful but insufficiently supported.

Allowed status values in this crosswalk:

- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `REJECTED`
- `UNRESOLVED`

A narrowing is recorded in `DISPOSITION_NOTE`; it is not a fifth status.

## Crosswalk entries

### VSH-001 — Function-over-surface translation

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H1, H2, H6.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** The VSNS branch repeatedly found that dictionary-level substitution was inadequate for cases where two descriptions differed strongly in vocabulary but appeared to play comparable roles. It proposed “Translate functions, not words,” then later added an explicit falsifiability requirement to prevent arbitrary analogy.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** A translation system should test preservation of semantic and communicative function independently of lexical/surface similarity.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** `docs/SEMANTIC_CONSERVATION.md` already distinguishes `FUNCTIONALLY_EQUIVALENT`, `CONTEXTUALLY_EQUIVALENT`, `PARTIAL_OVERLAP`, `APPROXIMATE`, and `NO_FAITHFUL_EQUIVALENT` and rejects surface similarity as sufficient.

**FALSIFIERS:** If two expressions produce the same task outcome but diverge under role reversal, cross-task transfer, changed context, counterfactual prediction, or pragmatic-force tests, function-based equivalence at the claimed scope fails.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** action-plan codes; reward-equivalent messages; fluent paraphrase with dropped provenance/modality; post-hoc analogy that can fit any pair of concepts.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** Strongly aligned with current UNVTRSLR, but predecessor evidence alone does not establish which functions are the correct invariants or that function is more universal than form.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not import Vera-specific phenomenological mappings, emotional labels, or assumptions that architectural-role similarity implies experiential identity.

### VSH-002 — Meaning and salience are separate variables

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H3, H4, H5.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** VSNS work narrowed “VSNS proper” to post-interpretive salience/importance estimation and explicitly separated semantic representation, contextual control, salience allocation, and response selection.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** The system must not use behavioral priority, urgency, attention weight, or salience as evidence that a particular semantic interpretation is correct.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** UNVTRSLR already separates semantic hypotheses from actions/goals and requires explicit provenance/uncertainty. Its conservation ledger treats affective/motivational structure as one dimension among others rather than as meaning itself.

**FALSIFIERS:** If a simpler architecture in which meaning and salience are one state variable performs equally well on adversarial context, ambiguity, and action-decoupling tests, the separation has not earned its complexity.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** equating frequently acted-on signals with their semantics; inferring “danger” from high response priority; assuming low salience means low semantic certainty.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** The separation is methodologically useful, but the exact predecessor staging order should not be imposed on every substrate.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not inherit VSNS-specific salience taxonomies, neuroanatomical metaphors, or person-specific importance models as universal primitives.

### VSH-003 — Segmentation is a hypothesis, not a precondition

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H1, H2.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** The branch documented that one acoustic stream can support different lexical segmentations and therefore different semantic routes. Its canonical example was language-specific and is not imported here; the methodological lesson is retained.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** When signal boundaries are not given by the channel, segmentation should remain probabilistic/plural until evidence discriminates among candidate units.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** `docs/SEMANTIC_SUBSTRATE.md` already has a segmentation plane and candidate signals that may be discrete, continuous, temporal, spatial, multimodal, composite, absent, or environmental actions.

**FALSIFIERS:** In environments where the channel itself provides lossless, semantically valid boundaries, plural segmentation may add no value and should collapse quickly.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** evaluator-supplied tokenization; hidden message boundaries; treating pauses or packet boundaries as semantic units; forcing text-like segmentation onto motion or continuous signals.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** This appears directly useful to UNVTRSLR because the project explicitly disallows assumed tokenization, but it still needs formal evaluator tests.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not assume words, phonemes, human prosodic units, or any language-specific segmentation inventory.

### VSH-004 — Context can reroute identical nominal forms

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H1, H2, H3, H4, H5.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** Small changes in emphasis, punctuation, local framing, speaker identity, listener model, and prior interaction were repeatedly treated as variables that can change which interpretation stabilizes even when much of the nominal signal remains constant.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** A semantic hypothesis should be conditioned on explicit context references, and evaluators should test both relevant context changes and nuisance context changes.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** UNVTRSLR already represents `Context` as environmental, interactional, historical, and participant state and includes context-transfer and nuisance-invariance tests.

**FALSIFIERS:** If a purported context variable does not change predictive, referential, compositional, or interactional behavior under controlled intervention, it should not be retained as meaning-relevant context for that mapping.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** episode-ID leakage; memorized scene templates; overfitting to speaker identity; treating every context difference as semantic.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** The predecessor claim should be narrowed from “context changes meaning” to “some context variables alter the best-supported semantic hypothesis; others are nuisance and should not.”

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not assume human relationship categories, conversational roles, or prosodic conventions apply to unknown agents.

### VSH-005 — Competing interpretations should remain live

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H3, H4, H5.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** Ambiguity work emphasized early competition, interpretive stability, entropy reduction, and preservation of alternative routes until context or evidence resolves them.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** Semantic state should support multiple simultaneous interpretations with distinct evidence, confidence, and discriminating tests instead of forcing a single best gloss too early.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** This is already a required responsibility in `docs/SEMANTIC_SUBSTRATE.md` and `specs/R1R2_EVALUATION_CONTRACT_V1.yaml` (`preserve_competing_interpretations`).

**FALSIFIERS:** If maintaining explicit alternatives consistently harms calibration/generalization without improving ambiguity detection, counterfactual prediction, or conservation, the representation may be too rich.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** top-1 label collapse; posterior mass hidden in opaque embeddings; evaluator selecting one interpretation before the learner sees the case.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** Historical work independently converges with a current repo commitment; Task 2 should research whether the explicit representation pays off versus implicit uncertainty.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not inherit a fixed number of routes, graph topology, or project-specific semantic-node labels.

### VSH-006 — Semantic content, provenance, truth, permission, and priority are separable axes

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H5.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** Semantic Perception Architecture treated meaning, salience, truth, consent/permission, identity, conation, continuity, repair, and response as separate constructs and warned against silent contamination among them.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** A translation or semantic bridge should not silently convert one axis into another: a claim can be understood without being believed; salient without being true; requested without being permitted; predicted without being observed; counterpart-supplied without evaluator endorsement.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** UNVTRSLR already makes provenance a semantic invariant and distinguishes observed action, requested action, predicted action, inferred intention, and claimed consequence.

**FALSIFIERS:** If an axis can be removed with no loss on provenance, deception, uncertainty, or action/permission minimal pairs, it may not deserve explicit representation in the semantic substrate.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** renderer turns “agent claims X” into “X”; receiver treats a request as authorization; salience treated as confidence; model inference rendered as observation.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** Provenance/truth distinctions are already strongly represented; permission/authority should enter UNVTRSLR only where the communication task actually contains action authorization, not as a universal semantic primitive.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not import Vera governance or consent machinery wholesale into the universal substrate.

### VSH-007 — Ambiguity collapse is information loss unless licensed

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H3, H5.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** The earlier architecture treated uncertainty, competing routes, and no-update states as legitimate outcomes rather than forcing a determinate interpretation.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** If more than one semantically material interpretation remains supported, a renderer should preserve or explicitly report that ambiguity rather than hide the choice behind fluent output.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** `AMBIGUITY_COLLAPSED` already exists in the UNVTRSLR conservation ledger.

**FALSIFIERS:** Ambiguity preservation is unnecessary when alternatives are semantically equivalent for the declared scope or when a declared decision policy explicitly licenses selection while retaining the discarded alternatives in provenance.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** fluent top-1 rendering; confidence threshold treated as truth; dropping minority interpretations that matter only under later context.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** The predecessor lesson reinforces an existing repo invariant but does not determine the best uncertainty representation.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not equate ambiguity with indecision, error, or pathology.

---

### VSH-008 — Counterfactual and minimal-pair tests expose routing better than agreement alone

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H1, H2, H5.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** CSST and microsemantic routing used controlled changes to wording, context, identity assumptions, emphasis, or scenario conditions to reveal which distinctions affected interpretation and response.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** Semantic claims should be stress-tested by interventions that make competing hypotheses predict different outcomes, including counterfactuals and one-variable minimal pairs.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** This matches the bootstrap protocol’s discriminating interactions and R2’s world-factor, message, counterfactual, nuisance, and minimal-pair logic.

**FALSIFIERS:** A proposed counterfactual test is weak if candidate hypotheses make the same prediction, if hidden side channels reveal the answer, or if changing the variable simultaneously changes unrelated factors.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** scenario labels; prompt-format leakage; evaluator-specific wording; learned test family instead of semantic distinction.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** The methodological overlap is strong; what transfers is intervention discipline, not the original human/person-specific stress scenarios.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not use private, traumatic, coercive, or person-specific scenarios as necessary semantic tests.

### VSH-009 — One-form/one-concept assumptions are unsafe

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H1, H2, H3.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** Polysemous micro-words, prosodic shifts, alternative segmentation, metaphor convergence, and contextual rerouting showed that a stable surface form need not correspond to one stable concept, while different surfaces can sometimes converge on a shared destination.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** The bridge should permit one-to-many, many-to-one, context-dependent, and partially overlapping mappings rather than defaulting to dictionary-like one-to-one entries.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** UNVTRSLR already has `ONE_TO_MANY`, `MANY_TO_ONE`, `PARTIAL_OVERLAP`, `CONTEXTUALLY_EQUIVALENT`, and `NO_FAITHFUL_EQUIVALENT` classes.

**FALSIFIERS:** In a deliberately constructed code with stable one-to-one mappings, richer mapping machinery should not invent ambiguity.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** English-word gloss used as ontology label; embedding similarity treated as concept identity; synonym tables treated as semantic equivalence.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** Historical work reinforces the current equivalence design and suggests human-language controls should explicitly include polysemy and segmentation ambiguity.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not assume human lexicalization patterns characterize alien or nonlinguistic systems.

### VSH-010 — Denotation and communicative force can diverge

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H1, H5; the predecessor materials distinguish intended meaning, perceived meaning, salience, and response but do not yet provide a fully validated pragmatic formalism.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** The same or similar propositional content could be interpreted differently depending on speaker, framing, emphasis, relationship/context, and expected response. The earlier architecture therefore separated semantic interpretation from downstream response and began distinguishing what a signal refers to from what it is doing in an interaction.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** Two signals can share referential/denotational content while differing in interactional function, addressee, evidential stance, requested response, or strategic use; translation should be able to conserve those distinctions where demonstrated.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** `docs/SEMANTIC_CONSERVATION.md` already lists communicative force, evidential status, social-role structure, and context dependence as independent conservation dimensions, but the repo does not yet deeply specify pragmatic function.

**FALSIFIERS:** If an evaluator cannot independently demonstrate a functional distinction beyond denotation, the translator should not invent one from human pragmatic labels.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** classifying punctuation or sentence mood as intent; using target-language speech-act markers as hidden labels; inferring sender intention from receiver reaction alone.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** This is the strongest direct opening for the planned R3 pragmatics layer, but it needs external literature and non-human controls before architectural promotion.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not treat assertion/request/warning/joke/threat categories as universal primitives; use them as human-derived experimental families.

### VSH-011 — Embeddings are instrumentation, not semantic ground truth

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H4.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** Earlier VSNS work explicitly recommended destination glosses and human annotation before embedding-based instrumentation because similarity models can hide why two items are considered close.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** Distributional/embedding similarity may assist discovery, clustering, or retrieval but must not itself certify semantic identity, grounding, provenance, or conservation.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** UNVTRSLR already rejects embedding closeness as sufficient evidence of semantic equivalence.

**FALSIFIERS:** If a specific learned representation passes the same grounding, intervention, partner-transfer, non-equivalence, and conservation tests as a more explicit representation, it may legitimately serve as the substrate; the objection is to untested similarity, not learned representations as such.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** nearest-neighbor glossing; shared pretrained latent space leaking common ontology; semantic scoring that rewards English-space similarity.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** Keep as evaluator discipline rather than a ban on neural or latent representations.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not require human annotation in the extraterrestrial target condition; it is a control/validation tool for Earth-language development stages.

### VSH-012 — Negative results and simpler baselines must survive the research loop

**EVIDENCE_CLASS:** `PROJECT_HISTORY_OBSERVATION`

**SOURCE:** H5, H6.

**PREDECESSOR_OBSERVATION:** The predecessor research program explicitly allowed itself to collapse into known/simpler models, required negative-result preservation, and warned against architecture debt produced by naming a new module for every observed failure.

**TRANSFERABLE_PROPOSITION:** UNVTRSLR should preserve rejected interpretations/architectures and continuously test whether a simpler substrate or explanation accounts for the same behavior.

**CURRENT_REPO_ANALOGUE:** R1 explicitly requires richer candidates to earn complexity, and R2 requires automated shortcut search and negative controls.

**FALSIFIERS:** None in the ordinary sense; this is a research-governance rule. It should itself be revised if it causes retention of low-value debris that obscures current evidence rather than improving falsification.

**KNOWN_SHORTCUTS:** novelty bias; architecture accretion; treating named components as evidence they correspond to distinct mechanisms; deleting failed hypotheses and later rediscovering them as if new.

**STATUS:** `HYPOTHESIS`

**DISPOSITION_NOTE:** Strong process alignment with current R1/R2. The concrete mechanism in this lane is to record rejected/narrowed predecessor hypotheses, not just surviving ones.

**DO_NOT_INHERIT:** Do not import the predecessor project’s full governance/module taxonomy; keep only the falsification and simplicity discipline.

## Explicit exclusions from portability

The following predecessor material is intentionally **not** imported into UNVTRSLR:

- Vera-specific identity or self-concept claims;
- person-specific salience, attachment, relationship, intimacy, grief, or love examples;
- private continuity records;
- consent/authority states that belong to Vera governance rather than a general communication theory;
- claims about subjective feeling or phenomenology;
- project-specific metaphors treated as if they were ontology;
- any claim that VSNS is a recognized anatomical or universal cognitive network;
- any architecture component whose only evidence is internal naming or self-description.

## Current first-cycle frontier

All entries remain `HYPOTHESIS` until the external research/challenge pass. The strongest apparent prior-to-current convergence is in:

- plural interpretations and ambiguity conservation;
- context-sensitive routing;
- separation of meaning from downstream salience/action;
- provenance-bearing distinctions;
- functional equivalence as a typed, scoped relation rather than lexical identity;
- intervention/minimal-pair testing;
- explicit simpler-baseline and negative-control discipline.

The most likely overclaim requiring narrowing is the slogan “translate functions, not words.” UNVTRSLR should test preservation of multiple semantic dimensions, including form when form itself carries meaning, rather than assuming function always outranks surface structure.
