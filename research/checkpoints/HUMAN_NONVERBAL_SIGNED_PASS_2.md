## Category boundaries preserved

Pass 2 does not use `nonverbal language` as a catch-all. It distinguishes:

1. established signed languages — full human linguistic systems;
2. homesign and emerging signed languages — evidence about structure under reduced conventional input and community/transmission effects;
3. silent gesture/pantomime — ad hoc or experimentally conventionalizing manual systems;
4. nonlinguistic human communication — pointing/gaze, facial/body behavior, touch, timing, and related channels where evidence supports analysis.

## Strongest architecture changes

1. A generic bootstrap cannot require a one-dimensional sequential token stream. Meaning-bearing structure may overlap across independently moving/manual/nonmanual channels.
2. `channel` and `environment` are not guaranteed cleanly separate. Physical space itself can become a linguistic/discourse resource, so renderer-normalized shared coordinates may be a semantic subsidy.
3. Physical form does not determine functional class. A point, gaze shift, facial action, trajectory, or body movement may be linguistic, paralinguistic, affective, interactional, or incidental depending on context.
4. Dyadic bridge formation, community conventionalization, and cohort/intergenerational transmission are different achievements and must not be collapsed.
5. Homesign supports strong human structuring capacities under reduced conventional language input, but contemporary scholarship contests important terminology, theory, and methodology; the repo does not present foundational interpretations as uncontested.
6. Visual/manual iconicity is treated as an accelerator/difficulty condition, never as an answer key.
7. The first 100 now admit simultaneous composition, multimodal meta-communication, negotiated spatial frames, non-object-centered rivals, and explicit dyad/community provenance.
8. Pass 2 strengthens the R0.5 split between information availability and semantic attribution: a cue may distinguish states while its communicative function remains unresolved.
9. Pose, gaze, sign-recognition, coordinate, or facial preprocessing can become Thirteen's semantic relay/bridge oracle. Modality preprocessing therefore belongs inside the mediation-trust/provenance audit.

## Claim ceiling

Human signed/nonverbal evidence supports:

- full linguistic organization without speech;
- spontaneous structured communication under reduced conventional language access;
- community-driven conventionalization and language emergence;
- simultaneous/multichannel human linguistic encoding;
- deictic/iconic routes that can aid human grounding;
- multimodal human communication across gesture, face, body, and touch.

It does **not** establish:

- visual gesture as a universal bootstrap medium;
- universal event order;
- universal pointing semantics;
- universal facial-expression meanings;
- universal spatial frames;
- universal iconicity;
- that a dyadic bridge must become a language;
- or that language-like structure is necessary for a successful semantic bridge.
