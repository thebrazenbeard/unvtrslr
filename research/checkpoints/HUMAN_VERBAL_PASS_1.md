## Strongest architecture changes

1. `shared observable environment` is no longer treated as automatically sufficient common ground.
2. Proposed `R0.5 INTERACTION_IDENTIFIABILITY / COMMON-GROUND AUDIT` precedes semantic promotion.
3. Literal zero-semantics self-description is replaced by the project hypothesis `self-demonstrating -> convention-forming -> recursively self-describing`.
4. Joint attention/gaze/pointing are optional human routes, not generic prerequisites.
5. Repair is promoted as an early functional target but must be inferred from interaction rather than supplied as a semantic label.
6. `DISCOVERED_CORRESPONDENCE` is separated from `NEGOTIATED_CONVENTION`, `MIXED_OR_ADAPTED_CONVENTION`, `INFERRED_EQUIVALENCE`, and `EVALUATOR_DEFINED_RELATION`.
7. The first 100 remain evaluator-described challenges and now admit alternative dependency paths rather than one developmental order.
8. Human iconicity, task scaffolding, shared attention, pretrained priors, evaluator segmentation, and co-training are explicit false-success surfaces.

## Current claim ceiling

Human verbal-language evidence supports the plausibility of ambiguity reduction, convention formation, repair, context-sensitive mapping, and later systematization under human conditions.

It does not establish:

- a universal semantic vocabulary;
- a universal first-100 order;
- necessary joint attention;
- universal discrete turns/messages;
- species-neutral iconicity;
- unique semantic identifiability;
- sufficiency of a shared physical world;
- or feasibility of a self-describing interstellar protocol from zero semantics.
