## Main changes

### 1. Representation neutrality

`operational partition` is no longer the default project primitive.

Use `VERIFIED_OPERATIONAL_RELATION`, with relation form explicitly recorded. The supported structure may be discrete, continuous, metric, dynamical, field-like, trajectory-based, probabilistic, many-to-many, or unresolved.

### 2. Boundary neutrality

The dyadic fixture may still use `agent`, but the generalized audit uses `ACCESS_INFLUENCE_SCOPE` only as a provenance handle over exposure/influence paths.

The scope may be overlapping, distributed, collective, time-varying, experimenter-declared, learner-discovered, mixed, or unresolved. It is not automatically a natural cognitive individual.

### 3. No-destructive-quotient refinement

The preservation control no longer requires a globally stable `source-side history` primitive.

The evaluator instead checks whether preprocessing irreversibly collapses learner-accessible exposure conditions whose distinction is required to discriminate live target-claim rivals, while later crediting the learner with recovering that lost distinction.

### 4. Applicability anti-evasion

`NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_OBSERVED_COMMUNICATION_SYSTEM` is replaced at the machine-status level by:

- `APPLICABLE_TESTED`;
- `STRUCTURALLY_INAPPLICABLE_IN_SCOPE`;
- `APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED`.

Inapplicability requires a representation-neutral prerequisite statement, non-circular evidence, a cross-system variant where coherent, a re-entry condition, and explicit coverage reduction. Candidate failure cannot itself establish inapplicability.

### 5. Semantic-status ladder

Provisional claim levels:

- S0 `OBSERVABLE_REGULARITY`
- S1 `VERIFIED_OPERATIONAL_RELATION`
- S2 `CROSS_SYSTEM_CORRESPONDENCE`
- S3 `SEMANTIC_CORRESPONDENCE_CANDIDATE`
- S4 `SEMANTIC_CORRESPONDENCE_SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE`

No automatic S5 / `TRUE_MEANING` level exists.

### 6. Semantic surplus

A semantic interpretation must add at least one preregistered, claim-discriminating empirical obligation beyond the strongest verified nonsemantic operational rival. Otherwise the ceiling remains `VERIFIED_OPERATIONAL_RELATION / HUMAN_RENDERING_ONLY`.

This is a project hypothesis to attack, not a solved definition of meaning.

## Current strongest bootstrap claim

> Under declared conditions where learner-accessible exposure contains recoverable distinguishing structure, preprocessing preserves claim-relevant distinctions, privileged instrumentation does not supply the cross-system bridge, and rival hypotheses can be discriminated within scope, initially unaligned systems may be able to construct reusable cross-system operational correspondences without a pre-shared symbolic language.

Whether the correspondence deserves the stronger label `semantic` remains a separate qualification question.
